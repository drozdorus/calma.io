Calma is a performance marketing company. We build and scale campaigns for the
US market across insurance, home services and other service verticals, running
on Meta, Google and TikTok. We launch hundreds of tests, automate a lot of it
with AI, and decide on data.

We're looking for a Creative Producer to raise both the speed and the quality of
our creative output.

## What you'll do

- Find new angles, hooks and advertising hypotheses
- Research competitors through the Meta Ads Library, spy tools and other sources
- Produce static, video and UGC creatives together with designers and creators
- Set tasks, keep production moving and take creatives all the way to finished
- Work with AI tooling to generate content and speed up production
- Analyse test results, spot the patterns and launch the next iteration
- Work with media buyers to find the creatives that scale

## What we expect

- 1–3 years in performance marketing, creative production or UGC
- Experience working with performance teams
- Experience with the US market
- Hands-on use of tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Veo, Arcads, Kling, Sora,
  ElevenLabs and CapCut

**Nice to have:**

- English at Upper-Intermediate or above
- Structure, ownership and a high working tempo
- The ability to break a strong creative into its parts and explain why it works
- A working understanding of advertising on Meta and TikTok

## What we offer

- Fully remote work
- Fast decisions, without layers of approval
- Real influence on the outcome — good ideas get launched immediately
- Large advertising budgets and a Tier-1 market
- Access to the best AI and marketing tools
- A team that likes experiments, speed and common sense
- Bonuses for strong results

## How we work

We test ideas quickly, decide on data, and don't complicate processes without a
reason. This role involves a lot of research, hunting for new approaches, working
with AI and running constant experiments. If that format appeals to you, you'll
be comfortable here.

What matters to us isn't a polished deck — it's strong hypotheses that open new
points of growth.

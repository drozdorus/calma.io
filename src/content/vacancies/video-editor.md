Calma is a performance marketing company. We build and scale campaigns for the
US market across insurance, home services and other service verticals, running
on Meta, Google and TikTok. We launch hundreds of tests, automate a lot of it
with AI, and decide on data.

We're looking for a Video Editor to make short vertical ad creatives for TikTok
and Meta. Creative is our main growth lever: every week we test batches of new
video, iterate the winners, and produce a growing share of the content through AI
generation.

## What you'll do

- Cut vertical ad creatives — hook, retention, jump cuts, zooms, subtitles,
  graphics, sound design — so the viewer doesn't drop at the second mark
- Iterate winners: new hooks on a working base, split styles, adaptations for
  new audiences and angles
- Generate source material with AI where no footage exists: scenes, hooks,
  characters
- Work in a pair with the creative strategist and media buyers: brief, feedback
  on the numbers, next iteration

## What we expect

- Confident editing in After Effects and Premiere Pro
- Experience cutting **advertising** creatives specifically (TikTok / Meta)
- An understanding of hook and retention, and the ability to explain your own
  editing decisions
- English good enough to work with English-language content and references

**A big plus:**

- Real experience with AI video generation: Veo, Kling, Runway, Sora, Seedance,
  Magnific, ElevenLabs — any of it, in actual work
- ComfyUI or other node-based generation pipelines
- An understanding of performance logic: what hook rate is, why a creative burns
  out, what iterating a winner looks like
- The habit of testing new AI services without being asked

We don't expect you to know every tool on the list. We expect to see that AI is a
real working instrument for you, not a line on a CV.

## What we offer

- Fully remote work
- Work with a modern AI video generation stack — at the front of where the
  industry is heading
- A direct line from your creative to performance numbers, no work into the void
- Access to leading tools and technology
- A team of like-minded people who like experiments, speed and common sense

## How the process works

1. **Application with a portfolio:** 3–5 advertising creatives you consider your
   strongest (links). If you work with AI, add 1–2 examples of generated or
   hybrid content — it speeds up the review. Applications without advertising
   creatives in the portfolio aren't considered.
2. **A call walking through the portfolio:** you show your creatives and explain
   the decisions — why that hook, how you held attention, what you'd redo now.
   How you think matters to us, not only what you render.
